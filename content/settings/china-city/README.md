# China City setting

Year: 2004. Excuse for being stuck without help: your bag was stolen with your passport and phone, and the police say to come back in a month.
Tech that exists: mobile phones, landlines, net cafés.
Tech that doesn't: smartphones, translation apps, the internet as a way out of learning the language.
Keep new scenes consistent with this: characters can call or be called, but nobody can look up a word for the player.
