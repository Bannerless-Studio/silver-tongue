# China City setting

Year: 1980. Excuse for being stuck without help: your bag was stolen with your passport, and the police say to come back in a month.
Tech that exists: landlines (in shops, offices, the station, the hall downstairs; not in your pocket or your room), television, radio, trains, planes, a few taxis.
Tech that doesn't: mobile phones, computers, the internet, anything that could look up a word for the player.
Keep new scenes consistent with this: characters can call or be called on a shared phone, but nobody can look up a word for the player.
